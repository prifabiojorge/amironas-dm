WHAT THESE VALUES MEAN FOR YOUR TESIS
======================================================================

IF Rg ≈ 15-20 Å:
  ✓ Protein is compact and native
  ✓ Ligand stabilizes protein structure
  ✓ Excellent for molecular docking studies
  ✓ Ready for mechanism of action studies

IF Rg ≈ 20-25 Å:
  ✓ Protein is normally compact
  ✓ Good structural stability
  ✓ Normal behavior expected

IF Rg ≈ 25-30 Å:
  ⚠ Check if protein is flexible
  ⚠ Might indicate conformational dynamics
  ⚠ Still acceptable for docking

IF Rg > 30 Å OR INCREASING TREND:
  ❌ Possible partial unfolding
  ❌ Ligand may not stabilize properly
  ❌ Check field force parameters
  ❌ Verify simulation quality
